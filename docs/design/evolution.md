# Evolution roster - decided

Each of Termon's 24 original monsters is the first stage of its own three-stage family. The roster therefore contains 72 original Species: 24 existing bases and 48 newly designed evolutions.

## Rules

- Evolution permanently changes a Monster's Species after it reaches the required level.
- Every current family has exactly three stages, one successor per stage, and no branches.
- Each family keeps one Type across all three stages so its matchup identity remains stable.
- Middle stages have a base-stat total of 320 and final stages total 400. Their distribution extends the base monster's role rather than flattening every final form into the same profile.
- Family roles and stat distributions are evaluated as complete three-Monster team counterplay under [Gameplay balance methodology](balance-methodology.md); an isolated Type-disadvantaged duel does not require individual parity.
- The content pack stores and validates Evolution rules. XP gain, Level progression, and the player-facing Evolution choice are implemented according to [Individual Monster progression](progression.md).

Stats are `hp / attack / defense / sp_attack / speed`.
